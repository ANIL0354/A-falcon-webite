Privacy policy

This is an overview of how A Falcon thinks about your information. Falcon is designed to keep your work private by default.

01Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run Falcon: account details you choose to provide, the content you add to the app, and basic technical information that keeps the service secure and reliable.

02How we use information

Information is used to provide the app, sync your workspace across devices, improve reliability, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information or use your private content for advertising.

03Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate Falcon, and only to the extent necessary. We may also disclose information when required by law.

04Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Sync between your devices is end-to-end encrypted, and sensitive sorting happens on your device wherever possible.

05Your choices

You can update your account details, export your data, or request deletion at any time by writing to us. You can also disconnect any integrated service without losing access to Falcon.

06Contact and updates

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@afalcon.club. We may update this policy as Falcon evolves and will post the current version on this page.
